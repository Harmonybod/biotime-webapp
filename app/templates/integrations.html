Attendance: GET /api/reports/attendance

Calculated from BioTime punches against the work schedule. Only completed days are counted: today is included once it's over, and calculated_through in the response shows the last day counted. Pick what you need with metrics; the groups worked, absence and late can be combined, or mixed with single field names.

Metrics

Every row also has emp_code, name and department. Hours are decimal (8.5 = 8h 30m). Day status is one of Present, Absent, On leave, Off day, Worked on off day.

Examples

Worked hours only, for everyone, for a month:

GET {{ base_url }}/api/reports/attendance?start_date=2026-10-01&end_date=2026-10-31&metrics=worked
X-API-Key: {{ example_key }}

Absence and late arrivals for one department:

GET {{ base_url }}/api/reports/attendance?start_date=2026-10-01&end_date=2026-10-31&metrics=absence,late&department=HR
X-API-Key: {{ example_key }}

With curl:

curl -H "X-API-Key: {{ example_key }}" "{{ base_url }}/api/reports/attendance?start_date=2026-10-01&end_date=2026-10-31&metrics=worked,late"

With PowerShell:

Invoke-RestMethod -Headers @{ "X-API-Key" = "{{ example_key }}" } -Uri "{{ base_url }}/api/reports/attendance?start_date=2026-10-01&end_date=2026-10-31&metrics=worked,late"
